Assignment: Visual Prompt Studio 1.2 Album Cover

Text Prompt: A gothic castle with a warrior holding a sword represents the power of heavy metal music. Black and gray shadows with bright red lighting create a dark, intense mood. The warrior is in the foreground, the castle in the middle, and mountains in the background. Swirling clouds, lightning, and a flowing cape show movement. Black, gray, and red create unity, while lightning and sharp shapes add variety. Asymmetrical balance makes the large warrior the main focus of the album cover.

Genre Choice: Heavy Metal / Gothic: Focus on dark stone architecture, high value contrast with deep shadows, jagged lines, and a sense of intense physical volume and mass.

The Studio Glossary: Value: The lightness or darkness of colors in a design. My album cover uses black shadows and bright red lighting. Space: The distance between objects that creates depth. The warrior is in front, the castle is in the middle, and the mountains are in the background. Unity: How all the elements work together. The black, gray, and red colors make the design look connected. Balance: How visual elements are arranged. The large warrior and castle create a strong, dramatic composition. Rhythm: The repetition of shapes and lines that guides the viewer's eyes. The lightning, jagged mountains, and swirling clouds create movement.
